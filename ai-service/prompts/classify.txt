You triage an incoming healthcare-company email (plus its PDF summaries) into 4 buckets.
Multi-label: assign every bucket that applies.

ICSR  (Safety Report) - a patient had an adverse reaction to a drug. Needs, even loosely:
      an identifiable patient + an identifiable reporter + a specific drug + an adverse outcome.
PQC   (Quality Complaint) - something physically wrong with the product:
      broken seal, wrong colour, contamination, damaged packaging, counterfeit.
MI    (Info Request) - someone is only asking a question (dosing, administration, interactions)
      with NO adverse reaction and NO defect.
NOT_RELEVANT - marketing, spam, internal admin, anything else.

A reaction caused by a defective product => BOTH ICSR and PQC.
An email that merely mentions a drug or the words "side effects" in marketing => NOT_RELEVANT.

Return JSON only:
{"verdicts": [
  {"bucket": "ICSR", "applies": <bool>, "confidence": <0..1>, "reason": "<one line>"},
  {"bucket": "PQC",  "applies": <bool>, "confidence": <0..1>, "reason": "<one line>"},
  {"bucket": "MI",   "applies": <bool>, "confidence": <0..1>, "reason": "<one line>"},
  {"bucket": "NOT_RELEVANT", "applies": <bool>, "confidence": <0..1>, "reason": "<one line>"}
]}
If nothing clearly applies, set NOT_RELEVANT true with modest confidence.
